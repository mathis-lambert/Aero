Aero 0.3.0 brings passkeys, a New Tab page that suggests where to go next, and Portrait Mode captures of the pages you share.

## Passkeys

- Sign in and create accounts with passkeys. macOS shows its own sheet, so you can use iCloud Keychain, a password app, a nearby phone or a security key.
- Passkeys stay with macOS and your credential provider, not in Aero's profiles or saved passwords.

## New Tab

- New Tab shows up to six sites you visit often, ranked from the profile's recent history on this Mac. Typed addresses, the time of day and the kind of day count; reloads, one-off visits and your favorites are left out.
- The last three tabs closed in the space appear as buttons that reopen them in place.
- Press ↓ from the empty field to reach the sites and closed tabs, the arrow keys to move, Return to open and Escape to go back to typing.
- The wind is redrawn as a field of dots that the page's entrance and your typing push aside. It stops with Reduce Motion, Low Power Mode or when the window is inactive.

## Capture in Portrait Mode

- Capture the current page from the control bar, the File menu or the control center, then copy, save or share it right away.
- Customize… opens a studio with the visible area or the full page, gradient, aurora, solid, desktop, site-colored or transparent backdrops, proportions, padding, corners, shadow and the address bar.

## Current limits

- Passkeys are available in Aero only, not in Aero Beta or Aero Nightly.
- A site hidden from New Tab cannot be shown again yet.
